The Light of Christ

"I am the light of the world." — John 8:12

Christ Brings Light

Throughout the New Testament and the Book of Mormon, Jesus Christ is described as the source of spiritual light. His teachings guide people out of darkness and into truth.

"I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life."
— John 8:12

The Light That Guides Us

The Book of Mormon teaches that Christ gives every person a measure of His light to help them recognize good and evil.

"For behold, the Spirit of Christ is given to every man, that he may know good from evil."
— Moroni 7:16

Walking in His Light

Following Christ means striving to live according to His teachings: loving others, seeking truth, and serving those in need.

As we follow Him, our lives gradually become brighter with hope and purpose.
